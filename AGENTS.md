# Repository rules

- Documentation, data analysis and validation must remain CPU-only. Do not allocate a cloud runtime for routine checks.
- The historical research round is closed with zero accepted main clips. Do not change failed/pending results into successful measurements.
- Never add login accounts, personal email, Drive IDs, runtime session identifiers, credentials, authorization logs, account balances or private archives.
- Keep upstream attribution and license notices. Do not add model weights or native binary caches.
- Public deployment scripts are adaptations, not GPU-revalidated releases. Their allocation opt-in is disabled by default.
- Distinguish measured data, rate-based estimates, documentation facts and future experiments. Do not claim an unverified promotion, Colab text-to-image success or large-scale throughput.
